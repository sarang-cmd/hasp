Product

One list, sealed with one password.

Hasp is a web application that runs on your machine. It holds keys, codes and notes, and it does not contact anything.

Open the demonstration

The vault

A list with a search field, a type filter and an environment filter. Selecting a row opens an inspector: the masked secret, its metadata, and the actions that apply to that item.

Create and edit
Name, service, host, type, environment, value and notes.
Reveal
Secrets stay masked until asked. Revealing is per item and per session.
Copy
The clipboard clears after 30 seconds, and the timer is cancelled when the vault locks.
Rotate
Replacing a value keeps the previous version, so a rotation is reversible.
Search
Queries match a blind index built from keyed hashes, so the vault never stores the words you searched for.

Two directions, four surfaces

Direction and mode are independent choices, and the accent is a third axis. That is four surfaces with one set of components, and the contrast floor is checked per combination by the test suite.

Nightshift, light

Foundry, dark

Nightshift is neumorphic: controls sit in the surface instead of on top of it, drawn with a paired light and dark shadow and no visible borders. Foundry is structural: square corners, one pixel rules, and a solid header. Both support black, white and neutral accent styles.

Import with review

Drop an export in and Hasp parses it into candidates, each with a confidence score. You review before anything is stored, and a parsed import is single use and expires after 30 minutes.

Notes
Google Keep JSON and HTML.
Tables

Codes
otpauth URIs, and the exports of Aegis, 2FAS, Raivo, Bitwarden, Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator.
