Cookies

Last updated 4 November 2026

One cookie

When you unlock the vault, the server sets a session cookie. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, scoped to the path, and it expires with the session.

It holds a random session identifier and nothing else. The vault key stays in the server's memory and is never written into the cookie.

Nothing else

There are no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no third-party cookies and no localStorage identifiers used for tracking. Appearance settings are stored in localStorage on your device and are never transmitted.

Why there is no banner

A consent banner is required for non-essential cookies. The session cookie is strictly necessary to keep the vault open, and nothing else is set, so there is nothing to consent to. If that changes, this page changes first.

Removing it

Locking the vault clears the cookie. Clearing your browser's site data does the same. Neither deletes the vault file.
